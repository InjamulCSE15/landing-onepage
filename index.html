Fast deployment and simple management

Managed ChromeOS devices see up to 76%-time savings during deployment compared to devices running other operating systems. The cloud-based Google Admin Console allows IT to configure more than 500 policies, control updates, and install or manage apps from anywhere.

Apps for Every Worker

ChromeOS provides access to popular productivity suites and video conferencing apps. The Chrome Enterprise Recommended badge highlights optimised apps for ChromeOS.

Modern Employee Experience

These devices boot up quickly and maintain their speed over time. Cloud profiles allow users to log in to any device and resume work seamlessly. Updates are installed in the background, facilitating work from anywhere.

Built-in and Proactive Security

ChromeOS offers advanced security controls. IT can disable lost Chromebooks to protect corporate data and set devices as ephemeral to remove user data after each session.

Eco-friendly Investment

Switching to Chromebooks can save $482 per device per year in deployment, upgrade, and training costs. They use up to 46% less energy and are made with sustainable materials.

Chrome Enterprise Upgrade

This upgrade enhances the business capabilities of ChromeOS devices, including 24/7 IT admin support, remote device management, advanced security features, update management, insightful reporting, and granular controls.

Device Options for Various Use Cases

Acer offers a range of ChromeOS devices suitable for different professional needs, including fieldwork, remote work, customer service, video conferencing, and more.
